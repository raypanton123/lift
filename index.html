Why this program

Volume. 10-20 hard sets per muscle per week. Growth climbs steeply to about 10, keeps going past 20 with shrinking returns, and under 4 does nothing.

Frequency. Twice a week per muscle. Per-session volume stops paying off past roughly 11 sets for one muscle, so splitting the week keeps every set hard.

Effort. 1-3 RIR on compounds. Last set of isolation work to failure. Failure is not required for growth, but genuinely hard sets are - most people stop far earlier than they think.

Loads. Mostly 6-12 reps. 5 to 30 all work if effort is high; this is the time-efficient middle.

Exercise choice. Where it has been tested head to head, loading a muscle stretched wins: seated leg curl +14% vs lying +9%, overhead triceps extension about 40% more growth than pushdowns, incline curl +11.2% at the upper biceps vs preacher.
